Chapter II

A place to come back to yourself.

HummingBeing began with a personal realisation: that real transformation lives in the body, not in another strategy or framework.

It was born from Isabelle’s own healing journey, through and out of the kind of environments that quietly deplete us. Through trauma-informed, mindfulness and neuroscience-backed practices, HummingBeing helps people release what they have been carrying and find themselves again: empowered, respected and as healthy as possible.

The practice is led by Isabelle Claus Teixeira, Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer and ICF PCC Executive Coach, a certified leadership coach since 2012, who is also a Strozzi Somatic Bodywork practitioner and a Reiki Level II practitioner.

Sessions run in person in Singapore and Japan, and online worldwide. HummingBeing is part of Business and Human Development Consulting Pte Ltd (BHD Asia).

Chapter XVI

The few rules that matter.

Check these before anything leaves the studio.

Do

  • Navy and gold on cream; navy only for bands, footers and event heroes.
  • One italic gold word per headline, in Playfair.
  • The all-gold wordmark with one wing-tip of clear space.
  • TRE™ in every visible line.
  • Real photos in natural light.
  • Outline buttons that fill from the pointer.
  • Write the contact as plain text: bonjour@bhdasia.com.
  • Use Simba’s Living with the Spirit logo whenever Simba appears on a material.

Don’t

  • Two-tone the wordmark or separate the bird.
  • White text on gold.
  • The registered mark or a bare TRE in text.
  • Medical claims, hype or urgency.
  • Emoji in headings.
  • Extra colours on posters (sage, terracotta).
  • Print discount codes on public art.
  • Use a personal address, or make the email a link.
  • Use the rainbow hummingbird: it belongs to Saymara and appears only on Saymara events.

ICF CCE badge. Use it only on ICF-accredited events. Credits are written “ICF CCEs”.
